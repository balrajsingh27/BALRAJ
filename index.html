Data and safety notes

Atmos provides general planning information based on weather and air-quality forecast models. Values can be delayed, incomplete or different from conditions at your exact location.

Use official local alerts for emergency decisions. Farm prompts are not crop-specific professional advice and cannot determine irrigation needs, pesticide suitability, or harvest readiness by themselves. Follow local agricultural guidance and all product labels.

Your saved app preferences, tasks and usage summaries are stored in your browser. Using a weather search or current-location weather sends coordinates to Open-Meteo's public APIs to fetch forecast data.
